Questions

Everything you're probably wondering.

Is my client data secure? +
Yes. Your data is hosted in the UK and handled in line with GDPR, and we build to Cyber Essentials standards. Your clients' financial information stays private to your practice.
Can I bring my existing clients across? +
Yes — import your client list from a spreadsheet in a few minutes. No need to retype anything or start from scratch.
Do my clients have to download or log into anything? +
No. They get a simple email with a secure link to send their documents. No app, no account, no friction — which is exactly why they actually respond.
Does the free plan really stay free? +
It does. The free plan covers the core CRM and deadline tracking for up to 50 clients, with no time limit. The AI features — including the chasing engine — are on the Solo and Firm plans.
What if I use more AI than my plan includes? +
Your plan's fair-use allowance covers normal day-to-day use. Beyond that, extra usage is billed per action at a small rate, and you can always see where you are — no surprise bills.
Can Clera be tailored to how my firm works? +
Most practices run happily on the standard product. Larger firms that need something shaped to their exact workflow can commission a bespoke build — start with a free consultation.
